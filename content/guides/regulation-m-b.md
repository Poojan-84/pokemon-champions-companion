## What's a "regulation," again?

If you read our [Beginner Overview](/guides/beginner-overview), you already know the short version: a regulation is the current rule set — which Pokémon are allowed, and what restrictions apply. Regulations rotate every few months, so what's true today won't necessarily be true in a few months. This page covers the **current** regulation. If something here feels out of date, it's worth double-checking — that's the nature of a live competitive format.

## The current regulation: M-B

Regulation M-B runs from **June 17 to September 2, 2026**.

<div class="callout">
<p><strong>The headline news for M-B: there are no banned Pokémon this season.</strong> If a Pokémon exists in the current Champions roster, you can use it — Mega Evolutions included.</p>
</div>

That's unusually open compared to past regulations in other Pokémon games, where a handful of the strongest Pokémon are often restricted.

M-B also introduced **22 new Pokémon and 16 new Mega Evolutions** compared to the previous regulation, which is part of why the [Tier List](/tier-list) looks the way it does right now — a lot of new options entered the format at once.

## Team-building rules to know

A few rules apply no matter which Pokémon you pick:

- **One of each species** — your team can't include two Pokémon that share the same Pokédex number
- **One Mega Evolution per battle** — you can bring more than one Mega Stone on your team, but only one Pokémon can actually Mega Evolve in a given battle
- **No duplicate held items** — two Pokémon on your team can't hold the same item as each other

These rules matter when you're building a team using our [Pokédex](/pokedex) — if you're eyeing two Pokémon that both want the same item, or two Mega Evolutions, you'll need to make a choice.

## This page will change

Regulations don't last forever — M-B is scheduled to end September 2, 2026. When a new regulation begins, we'll update this page rather than leave stale information up. If you're reading this after that date, treat the dates above as historical, not current, and check for an update.
